Welcome to Choral Guild Kansas City!

We're thrilled to get our first season off the ground in Spring 2026, and we can't wait to introduce you to the team. Earlier this year, we received our official 501(c)(3) nonprofit status, marking an exciting milestone in our journey to bring choral music to life in Kansas City.

Join us as we build something new — a home for singers and audiences who believe in the joy of community and the transformative power of song.

Our Mission

To enrich Kansas City's cultural landscape through choral performance, education, and artistic collaboration.

Our Vision

We envision a vibrant community where voices unite to inspire, heal, and connect.
